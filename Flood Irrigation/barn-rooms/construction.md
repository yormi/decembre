### Germination room walls

- **Germination room** 1—2 **new wall** (154 in + 116 in) ; 1—2 **existing wall**

- **New wall** → sits on 7 in high × 5,5 in deep concrete mini-wall


### Office floor

- Floor bounce ← joist span × joist spacing × joist depth   (longer span / wider spacing / shallower joist → more bounce)

- Floor stiffness ← glued subfloor + mid-span blocking   (both raise stiffness; neither changes the joist span)

- Plywood end joint ← joist or block   (every 4 ft edge carried; T&G long edges carried by the tongue between joists)

- Office floor live load: 40 lb/ft² (1,9 kPa, house floor)
