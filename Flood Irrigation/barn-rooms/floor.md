# Barn rooms floor — office floor over the 3 rooms (~29 × 14 ft, ~400 ft²)

Plywood on the ceiling joists of the 3 side-by-side rooms. Rooms: `../../domain/barn-rooms.md`. Joists: `construction.md`.
Germination room joists: `../germination-room/germination-room.md` § Framing.

## Joists

- Cold room: new 2×6 × 12 ft beside every existing joist, bearing on both walls → adhesive bead
  → 3 in screws, 2 every 16 in, staggered.

- Blocking: 2×6, rows every ~4 ft across all 3 rooms (cold 2 rows, germination 2, graft 1),
  one block per bay, end-screwed.

## Plywood

- Sheets across the joists, T&G, end seams staggered.

- Every sheet end on a joist or a 2×6 block; long edges carried by the tongue.

- Bead of subfloor adhesive on every joist → screw 6 in on edges, 12 in in the field.
