# Barn rooms — build steps

When: `timeline.md`.


## Rodent closure, barn bathroom

- Nail ½ in plywood on the outer face of the bathroom walls, sheets butted tight

- Steel wool + silicone at every gap ≥ ¼ in: top, ends, bottom, cable + pipe holes

Spec: `../barn-rooms/rodent-closure.md`.


## Barn-side cladding

- Add blocking at 8 ft

- Nail ½ in plywood, vertical

- Screw battens over seams + every 16 in

Spec: `../barn-rooms/cladding.md`.


## Office floor

- Sister 10 cold-room joists, glued + screwed

- Block every ~4 ft, all 3 rooms

- Glue + screw 15 sheets ¾ in T&G

Spec: `../barn-rooms/floor.md`.
