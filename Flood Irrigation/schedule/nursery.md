# Nursery — build steps

When: `timeline.md`, island: `next-priorities-timeline.md`.

- **Zones**: south = 5 spots below the corridor · east = 4 spots on the east wall · rest = 11 spots (west wall 4, north wall 1, island 6)

- **P1 racks, 14**: 8 existing → south 5 + west/north 3 · 6 new → east 4 + west/north 2

- **Island, 6**: new, later

- **Always**: ≥ 8 lit racks in the nursery for production

- **Stages**: `timeline.md`


## Fill-drop tubes

- Cut 79 tubes, 1,4 m → elbow on one end (`../tray-fill-drop.md`)


## Collector ends

- Dig the north wall trench: bottom of both 3 in ABS at 10 in deep at the bucket

- Lay 2 × 3 in ABS on sand, 1 % fall to the bucket → cap the upstream ends for the east + west collectors

- Island + south branch end: dig → lay 3 in ABS on sand, 1 % fall to the box → cap

Spec: `../nursery/waste.md`.


## Rack power

- Install 7 two-pole 15 A breakers

- Pull 10 AC90 14/3 runs, panel → ceiling, 1 per rack pair: west 2 · north + east 1 · east 1 · east + south 1 · south 2 · island 3

- West + north: mount 5 ceiling boxes → jumpers → 5 GFCI

- South + east + island: coil each run at its rack spot, connected later

Spec: `../nursery/rack-lighting.md` § Electrical.


## Nursery floor, rest zone + corridor

- Level soil, rest zone + corridor

- Spread stone dust 2,5 cm → slabs, 4 ft level

- Fit slabs around the floor-drain collar, below the heater

Needs: rented wet masonry saw, 14 in.


## West + north collector run

- Glue the run from its capped end, 3 in ABS, 1 % fall, tee + stub per rack spot, cleanout at the run end

Spec: `../nursery/waste.md`.


## East racks → west + north

- Roll the 3 east racks onto 3 west + north spots, plants on → plug into ceiling GFCI


## First rack

- Team plumbs one west + north rack first, plants on → Richard copies it (`samples.md`)


## Tray ports

- Drill + fit drain and overflow ports, 63 trays: 40 existing racks first, then 23 new (`../tray-ports.md`)

- Drill 3/8 in grommet hole + fit grommet, 63 trays


## Drain-drop pre-assembly

- Teflon → adapter → valve → adapter, 63, copy the first rack


## Downpipes

- Glue 14 downpipes, copy the first rack: tees per tray tier → bushings → adapters

- Prefab 14 horizontal lines: elbows, stubs, tier-1 tees

Needs: Canac sanitary tees (22).


## South zone

- Roll the 5 south racks onto the corridor, plants on → level soil → stone dust → slabs → racks back

Needs: rented wet masonry saw, 14 in.

- Glue the island + south branch from its capped end, tee + stub per rack spot, island spots included, cleanout at the run end

- Connect the 2 south runs + the east/south shared run → 5 ceiling boxes → 5 GFCI → plug each power bar


## Existing racks, flooding, 8

- Per tier: lift the flats → set tray → shim (pour 5 L, drains to port) → flats back

- Zip-tie downpipes to posts

- Fit drain drops: vinyl → pre-assembled valve → vinyl → tee

- Fit overflow drops

- Fit spa-flex horizontal lines → collector tees


## Nursery supply + fertigation station

- Plumb bib → dual check → fertigation station → 10 psi → mains

- Plumb water-only line → week-1 valve

- Lay 5 zone lines with Orisha valves, all 14 rack spots

- Fit rack fill connections: tee → vinyl → cam-locks → sub-header + cap, 8 existing racks

- Punch fill drops into sub-headers, 8 existing racks → seat tubes

- Set up stock A drum + stock B bucket

Spec: `../nursery/water-supply.md`, `../nursery/fertigation-station.md`.


## Nursery control wiring

- Mount 2 Orisha units, 6 relays, contactor, PSU

- Wire drain valves, 8 existing racks, 18/2 + WAGO

- Place 4 tensiometers

Needs: Orisha 18/2 wire (300 ft).


## Nursery commissioning

- Leak-test every zone

- Time one drop per zone → set fill timers

- Program 5 zones


## East zone

- Glue the east collector run from its capped end, tee + stub per rack spot, cleanout at the run end

- Connect the east runs → 4 ceiling boxes → 4 GFCI
