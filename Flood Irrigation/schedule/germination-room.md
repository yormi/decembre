# Germination room — build steps

When: `timeline.md`.


## Germination room framing

- Frame each wall flat on a 7 in block bed beside the mini-wall, ½ in short, door opening

- Hidden-side wall: square → nail ½ in plywood → flip, plywood down (`../germination-room/rodent-closure.md`)

- Lay sill gasket on the mini-wall → tilt each wall up onto it → plumb + brace

- Tapcon the bottom plates → shim + screw the top plates to the barn frame

- Set ceiling joists + rims

Spec: `../germination-room/germination-room.md` § Framing.


## Germination room power

- Run 5 circuits 14/2 → boxes → breakers


## Germination room heater circuit

- Pull AC90 14/2 → heater can → 2-pole 15 A breaker; white re-marked red at both ends


## Germination room insulation + poly

- Insulate walls + ceiling, R-20

- Staple poly → seal laps + box boots


## Germination room lining

- Screw Trusscore walls + ceiling → trims

- Fit 5 receptacles + plates


## Germination room climate

- Cut the lining at the 4 framed openings

- Mount 2 exhaust fans high → barn; 4 back-draft dampers low, 2 per cavity

- Staple ¼ in steel mesh over the barn side of each opening

- Mount the wall heater in its can

- Set heater ~19 °C, fans ~23 °C

Spec: `../germination-room/climate.md`.
