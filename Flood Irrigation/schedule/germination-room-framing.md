# Germination room framing — what matters

Read before framing. Steps: `germination-room.md`. Spec: `../germination-room/germination-room.md`, `../../domain/barn-rooms.md`, `../barn-rooms/construction.md`.


## Layout grid


### One 16 in grid, one zero point

- Studs + ceiling joists at 16 in o.c.

- Pick one zero point per wall (the corner where cladding + lining start) → measure every stud from it, never stud to stud

- Same zero for both faces of the wall: cladding outside, lining inside

- 16 in serves every layer:

    - cladding: 4 ft sheet edges land on stud centres (48 = 3 × 16)

    - battens: every 16 in into studs

    - lining: each 16 in Trusscore board flange lands on a stud

    - insulation: R-20 batts are 15 in wide, sized for 16 in o.c.

    - office floor: joists at 16 in, sheet ends on a joist


### Corners

- Backing on both faces at every corner, new–new and new–existing

    - inside: cove corner trim + board ends screw into it

    - outside: cladding sheet edge + batten land on it

- 3-stud corner or ladder blocking; leave a gap for insulation


## Height


### Set by the office floor, not by the 10 ft

- Top of germination joists = top of the existing cold-room joists → office floor flat across the 3 rooms

- Measure that height from the slab first

- Stud length = joist top − 5½ in joist − 3 in double top plate − 1½ in bottom plate − 7 in mini-wall

- At 10 ft finished ceiling: ~109 in studs (cut from 10 ft)

- Ceiling height is the result; if it lands far from 10 ft → stop, ask


## Tilt-up

- Frame flat on a 7 in block bed beside the mini-wall → bottom plate swings onto it

- Frame ½ in short of the barn frame / joist line → top clears while tilting → shim after

- On the floor: AC90 holes, Tapcon holes in the bottom plate, blocking (8 ft seam, boxes, fan + intake + heater openings, fan controller)

- Hidden-side wall (150 ft²): square → ½ in plywood, 6 in edges / 12 in field → flip plywood-down → tilt up; ~425 lb, 3 people

- Plywood = wall bracing + rodent closure on that face (`../germination-room/rodent-closure.md`)

- Temporary diagonal braces until joists are on


## Walls on the mini-wall

- Sill gasket under the bottom plate

- Tapcon ¼ × 2¾ in, 24 in o.c., 6 in from each plate end; masonry bit + hammer drill

- Closed-off door opening: mini-wall poured + cured before plating over it

- Double top plate: lap the joints ≥ 4 ft, second plate overlaps corners

- Top plate → barn frame, 3 in structural screws (cladding spec)


## Ceiling joists + office floor


### Joists

- 2×6, across the 116 in span, bearing on the 154 in walls

- In line with the cold-room joists: same positions along the 154 in → office floor sheet ends land on a joist in both rooms

- On the top plate behind a 2×6 rim; toe-nail to the plate + nail through the rim

- Crown up, every joist


### Floor stiffness

- 2 blocking rows across the germination room, ~every 4 ft, one block per bay → in line with the cold-room rows

- Subfloor adhesive on every joist + block before the plywood

- Plywood: ¾ in T&G across the joists, end seams staggered, every end on a joist or block


### Ceiling below

- Trusscore ceiling boards run along the 154 in, across the joists → screw into every joist

- Blocking rows sit flush with the joist bottoms → no bumps in the ceiling


## Door

- In the 116 in wall → non-bearing (joists run parallel to it) → flat 2×6 header on the jacks

- 36 × 80 in man door; get the actual door + frame before framing → rough opening from it (prehung: ~38 × 82½ in)

- King + jack each side

- Blocking around the whole opening → backing for the J-trim

- Threshold under the kept door (sand mix) poured before the jambs

- Cripple studs above the header on the 16 in grid → lining board over the door ("door wall −2 + 1 cut in 3")

- Square, plumb jambs → J-trim sits straight


## Heating + cooling


### Heat balance

- Target: soil 18–21 °C until emergence; > 24 °C → lettuce thermo-dormancy (`../../domain/propagation.md`)

- 5 racks, lights on from day 3 after seeding, 05:00–23:00

- Heat in, lights on: 5 × 1,05 kW = 5,25 kW

- Heat out: ~20 W per °C between room + barn (R-20 walls + uninsulated slab) → ~0,6 kW at a −10 °C barn

- Lights on → ventilation every lit day, all year

- Days 1–3 (lights off) + nights → heat only

- No AC: barn > 24 °C → germination days run above thermo-dormancy


### Ventilation (lit days)

- Flow = 5,25 kW ÷ (room 21 °C − barn air)

    - barn −10 °C → ~300 CFM

    - barn 5 °C → ~580 CFM

    - barn 10 °C → ~840 CFM

    - barn 15 °C → ~1 540 CFM

- 2 × 12 in wall shutter fans, 1 920 CFM, built-in thermostat controllers → hold 21 °C up to a ~16 °C barn (`../germination-room/climate.md`)

- Fans draw 1,2 A together → plug into a rack outlet; no dedicated circuit

- Exhaust: 2 cavities high on one wall → barn; intake: 2 cavities low on the opposite wall, 2 × 10 in back-draft dampers each

- Frame all 4 openings now: header + sill blocks between 2 studs

- ¼ in steel mesh over both openings

- Poly sealed to both sleeves


### Wall heater

- Fan-forced wall heater, 1 500 W, 240 V → 6,25 A → 2-pole 15 A breaker + AC90 14/2

- Sized for lights-off days in deep winter: ~0,6 kW at a −10 °C barn, ~0,9 kW at −25 °C

- Recessed can between 2 studs at 16 in → frame the opening now, low on the wall, clear of the racks

- Front clearance per the heater manual; keep racks + flats out of the air stream

- Setpoints: heater ~19 °C, fan ~23 °C → never run together


### Envelope

- R-20 on every wall, incl. the shared wall with the cold room → protects the cold room as much as this one

- R-20 in the ceiling unless the office above is heated

- Door: insulated, weather-stripped

- Panel: 1 more 2-pole space (heater) → check before pulling cable


## Electricity

- 5 outlets, one per germination rack → mark rack positions before framing

- Box on the side of a stud, face ½ in proud of the stud face → flush with Trusscore

- AC90 holes centred in the stud, ≥ 1¼ in from either face; closer → steel plate

- Strap within 300 mm of each box + every 1,5 m

- Vapour boot on each box → leave room around the box for the poly lap


## Cladding (barn side)

- Sheets vertical: 4 × 8 ft + 2 ft top strip

- Blocking row at 8 ft above the sheet bottom → horizontal seam lands on it

- Vertical seams on stud centres (16 in grid)

- Nail 6 in on edges, 12 in in the field → the plywood braces the walls

- No vapour barrier behind the plywood


## Lining (room side)

Source: Trusscore Wall&CeilingBoard installation guide.

- Vertical boards on vertical studs at 16 in: allowed, no strapping → board seams land on the studs

- Walls: top of mini-wall → ceiling, ~113 in

- Screw every 24 in along the flange, ≥ 1 in into the stud, centre of the slot, not tight (1/16 in under the head)

- Screws: stainless #8 pan head, 1½ in

- Gaps: ¼ in at both board ends + every trim end

- Ceiling: boards across the joists → screw at every joist

- Humid room: silicone bead in each tongue; ¼ in drain holes in the bottom J-trim; silicone at corners + around boxes

- Trims: J-trim at the bottom + around the door; cove on vertical corners + the wall–ceiling joint

- Silicone the wall–ceiling cove

- Acclimatize boards + trims 24 h in the room before installing

- Insulation + poly before any board; poly lapped + sealed at the plates
