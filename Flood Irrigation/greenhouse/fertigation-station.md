# Greenhouse fertigation station — manifold tee → check valve (week 5)

Sits between a new tee on the pressurized pump manifold and the 10 psi regulator
(`water-supply.md`). Pressurized only during a fill.

## Path

- pump manifold tee
→ Orisha fill valve
→ Dosatron A (1:10, stock A)
→ Dosatron B (1:100, stock B)
→ inline filter
→ check valve
→ 10 psi regulator (`water-supply.md`).

- Stock A + B: same recipes as the nursery station (`../nursery/fertigation-station.md`).
- Plain-water day: Dosatron bypass off.

## Parts

- **Manifold tee → Dosatron A in:**
    - manifold tee branch (¾ FPT; size: `../for-later.md`)
    → mamelon ¾ MPT × MPT
    → réduit 1 MPT × ¾ FPT
    → Orisha fill valve (1 FPT × FPT)
    → réduit 1 MPT × ¾ FPT
    → adaptateur mâle ¾ MPT × insert
    → ¾ PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → Dosatron A in (¾ MNPT)
- **Dosatron A → check valve (jumpers):**
    - Dosatron A out (¾ MNPT)
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → ¾ PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → Dosatron B in (¾ MNPT)
    - Dosatron B out (¾ MNPT)
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → ¾ PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → filter in (¾ MNPT)
    - filter out
    → clapet anti-retour (screws straight in)
