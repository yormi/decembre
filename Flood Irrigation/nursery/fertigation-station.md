# Nursery fertigation station — 50 psi regulator → filter (weeks 2–4)

Sits in the bib PE between the dual check and the main (`water-supply.md`). Feeds
zones wk 2–4; the week-1 water-only line tees off upstream of it (`water-supply.md`).

## Path

- **Station:**
    - bib PE
    → PSR-2 50 psi
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → Dosatron A (1:10, stock A)
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → Dosatron B (1:100, stock B)
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → PE jumper
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → inline filter
    → adaptateur femelle ¾ FPT × insert
    → PE
    → 10 psi regulator on the main (`water-supply.md`)

- Stock A: gypsum 2,0 g/L + iron sulfate; gypsum saturation ~2,5 g/L forces the 1:10.
  200 L lidded drum, 7-day batch (~400 g gypsum / 200 L, stir warm). ~25–29 L/day.
- Stock B: Ocean + Acadie + K sulfate 29 g/L + Mg sulfate + MicroStock, all soluble at
  100×. 20 L bucket, mixed daily. ~2,5–3 L/day.
- Ca never meets fish P at stock concentration.
- Plain-water day: Dosatron bypass off. Dosing is proportional to flow, so zone valves
  downstream are fine; keep any zone under 53 L/min.
- Verify heated-line pressure ≥ 0,5 bar at the injectors.
