# Nursery waste — downpipe foot → settling-box spot (weeks 1–4)

## Path

- Upstream: Each rack's glued downpipe → horizontal line (`rack-water.md`).
- 3 Collectors runs along each rack row intercepted by upstream sanitary tee to connect racks:
    - West & North wall
    - East wall
    - Island & South Wall
- Into the settling box: west + east collectors via 2 pipes in the north wall trench; island + south its own branch.
- Downstream: collectors end at the settling-box spot `floor-drain.md`.
