# Domain — nursery barn rooms


## Model


### Layout

- **Barn rooms** 1—3 **room**: **germination room**, **cold room**, **graft room**, side by side; cold room in the middle

- **Barn rooms** 1—1 **office floor** (second floor, ~29 × 14 ft, over all 3 rooms)

- **Barn rooms** 1—1 **barn-side cladding** (375 ft², barn-facing walls of the 3 rooms)


### Rooms

| Room | Inside | Ceiling joists | Joist spacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germination room | 154 × 116 in | 2×6, new, across the 116 in span | 16 in |
| Cold room | 154 × 134 in | 2×6, 12 ft long, existing, across the 134 in span, on 2×6 walls | 16 in |
| Graft room | 154 × 77 in | existing, across the 77 in span | 16 in |


### Germination room

- **Germination room** 1—2 **new wall** (154 in + 116 in) ; 1—2 **existing wall**

- **New wall** → sits on 7 in high × 5,5 in deep concrete mini-wall

- Height: 10 ft to the finished ceiling

- 1 man door, 36 × 80 in


### Office floor

- Floor bounce ← joist span × joist spacing × joist depth   (longer span / wider spacing / shallower joist → more bounce)

- Floor stiffness ← glued subfloor + mid-span blocking   (both raise stiffness; neither changes the joist span)

- Plywood end joint ← joist or block   (every 4 ft edge carried; T&G long edges carried by the tongue between joists)

- Office floor live load: 40 lb/ft² (1,9 kPa, house floor)
