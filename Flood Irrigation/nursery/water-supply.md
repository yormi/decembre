# Nursery water supply — bib → fill valves (weeks 1–4)

## Path

- **Feed line (weeks 2–4):**
    - bib (¾ MHT)
    → accouplement pivotant ¾ FHT × FPT
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → ¾ PE (any length)
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → dual check
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → ¾ PE (water-only tee)
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → fertigation station (`fertigation-station.md`)
    → ¾ PE 10 pi, 2 coudes
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → PSR-2 10 psi (pins the header at 0,7 bar)
    → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
    → main ¾ PE, 2 coudes
    → tee splitting 2 mains: north + west wall (50 pi) · east wall (10 pi)
    → wk 2 on the north + west main end, wk 4a on the east main end; wk 3 + wk 4b on
      take-off tees (zone table).
- **Water-only line (week 1):**
   - tee in the bib PE
   → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
   → own PSR-2 10 psi
   → adaptateur ¾ MPT × insert
   → ¾ PE 15 pi
   → ends at the week-1 Orisha valve (§ Zone lines)
- GHT threads half-way into NPT and leaks — never mix them.
- Buried ¾ PE 6 po deep, 2 pi inside the exterior wall is safe at 15 °C 24/7; drain the
  line (unscrew the end bouchon) if heat goes down in winter.
