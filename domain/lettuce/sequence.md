# Domain — lettuce nursery sequence


## Model


### Canopy closure on the bench

- leaf area per plant × density → LAI ; LAI 3 → canopy closed (light-limited)

- closure day ← density only, not pot size (LAI dynamics are per-area)

- leaves touch ~day 19 packed (32-pot 2,5 po) ; closed canopy → growth per-area, not per-plant

- respace → LAI halves/thirds instantly → canopy re-closes after a re-fill lag

- ×2 respace at day 19 → re-closes ~day 27–29 (DLI 21)

- packed 1 tray 32 pots → ×2 checker at leaf-touch → 2 trays 16 pots

### Densities — bench stages vs field configs

| Configuration | Plants/m² | Area per plant | Open until | Fresh weight at closure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bench packed (32-pot) | 210 | 48 cm² | ~day 19 | 11 g |
| Bench ×2 (16/tray) | 105 | 95 cm² | ~day 28 | 22 g |
| Bench ×3 (10–11/tray) | 70 | 143 cm² | ~day 32 | 33 g |
| Bench ×4 (8/tray) | 52 | 191 cm² | ~day 34 | 44 g |
| Bench ×5 (6–7/tray) | 42 | 238 cm² | ~day 35 | 55 g |
| Bench ×6 (5–6/tray) | 35 | 286 cm² | day 35+ | 66 g |
| Field 4 rangs × 6 po | 34 | 290 cm² | ~day 34 | 67 g |
| Field 3 rangs × 6 po | 26 | 388 cm² | ~day 35 | 90 g |
| Field 3 rangs × 8 po | 19 | 516 cm² | ~day 36 | 119 g |

- Fresh weight at closure = LAI 3 × area per plant ÷ SLA ÷ 7 % dry matter — proportional to area (11 g per ×1 of bench area)

- Field open-until assumes transplant at day 28 ; days count from sowing (DLI 25 in field)

- Bench ×6 = field density (4 rangs × 6 po) — the bench becomes pre-paid field area

- Bench ×3 = 2× field density → holds an open canopy about half as long
