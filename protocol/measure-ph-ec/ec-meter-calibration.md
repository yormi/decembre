## Why

Fertigation salts foul the graphite tips and cause slow, low readings.


## What you need

- 2.77 mS/cm (1413 µS on the KCl scale) solution

- Big bottles of calibration solution opened repeatedly absorb CO₂ and go stale — that ruins accuracy far more than brand ever will.

- Bluelab probe cleaning kit (or just a soft brush + the cleaning solution). 


## How

- 2 min

1. Rinse the probe
2. Sit it in solution
3. Hold the button until it locks.

- Recalibrate monthly, or any time a reading surprises you.
