# Greenhouse fertigation station — tank → fill valve (week 5)

Sits between the warm tank and the Orisha fill valve (`water-supply.md`).

## Path

warm tank → foot strainer → Franklin Turf Boss 1,5 HP (56 psi shutoff, self-priming,
warm water OK; inline pressure controller starts/stops it on demand, no dead-head) →
Dosatron A (1:10, stock A) → Dosatron B (1:100, stock B) → inline filter → check valve →
PSR-2 10 psi → fill valve.

- Stock A: gypsum 1,4 g/L + iron sulfate; 200 L drum, 7-day batch. Stock B: Ocean +
  Acadie + K sulfate + Mg sulfate + MicroStock; 20 L bucket, daily. Same recipes as
  the nursery station (`../nursery/fertigation-station.md`).
- Line floats ~3–3,5 bar; the PSR-2 pins the header at 0,7 bar. ~53 L/min = 14 GPM,
  inside the PSR-2 range; Senninger 1 po starts at 20 psi, so bush ¾ into the 1 po line.
- Water-hammer arrestor or flex section between the fill valve and the injectors.
- Plain-water day: Dosatron bypass off.
