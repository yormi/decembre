# Nursery fertigation station — dual check → 10 psi regulator (weeks 2–4)

Sits in the bib PE between the bib adapter and the main (`water-supply.md`). Feeds
zones wk 2–4; the week-1 water-only line tees off upstream of it. Phase 2: phase 1
runs the bib PE straight onto the PSR-2.

## Path

bib PE → dual check → PMR 50 psi → Dosatron A (1:10, stock A) → Dosatron B (1:100,
stock B) → inline filter → PSR-2 10 psi → main.

- Stock A: gypsum 1,4 g/L + iron sulfate; gypsum saturation ~2,5 g/L forces the 1:10.
  200 L lidded drum, 7-day batch (~280 g gypsum / 200 L, stir warm). ~25–29 L/day.
- Stock B: Ocean + Acadie + K sulfate 29 g/L + Mg sulfate + MicroStock, all soluble at
  100×. 20 L bucket, mixed daily. ~2,5–3 L/day.
- Ca never meets fish P at stock concentration.
- Plain-water day: Dosatron bypass off. Dosing is proportional to flow, so zone valves
  downstream are fine; keep any zone under 53 L/min.
- Verify heated-line pressure ≥ 0,5 bar at the injectors.
