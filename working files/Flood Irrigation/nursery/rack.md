# Rack — everything in one nursery rack (5 tray tiers, 6 shelves)

One rack = shell + 5 flooded tiers + one downpipe into the shared collector.
Multiply by rack count (`../project.md`). Shared kit: `water-supply.md`
(bib → fill valves), `waste.md` (collector → sewer). Port design:
`../tray-ports.md`. Fill-drop design: `../tray-fill-drop.md`.

| Poussière de pierre, sac 30 kg (quincaillerie) | 1 | |
| [Néoprène 1/16 po × 2 po × 10 pi](https://www.amazon.ca/Rubber-Sheet-Warehouse-Neoprene-Commercial/dp/B076MGHJNT) | 1 per 6 racks | cut 2 × 2 po pads, ~10/rack |
| [Néoprène 1/8 po × 5 po × 10 pi](https://www.amazon.ca/Neoprene-Rubber-Sheet-Rolls-Strips/dp/B016C5G5SM) | 1 per 12 racks | cut 2 × 2 po pads |

Casters replace the levelling feet (stem mount, either/or); shims take the last 1–2 cm.
Pads go under the high tray corners: pour 5 L, shim until it all runs to the drain port.

Slab under each post → rack roughly level → shim each tray, not the rack.
Never shim on bare soil: heaves in spring, settles under a flooded tray.
