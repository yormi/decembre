## Waste

- **Two 2 po ABS mains along the table, both to the floor
  drain (air gap above the grate).** Drain main: tray drain
  port → ¾ po vinyl → 2 × 2 × ¾ tee → 2 po main, ~1 % slope
  → 1 po full-port NO valve at the low point → floor drain.
  Overflow main: tray overflow port → ¾ po vinyl → tee →
  2 po main, unvalved, straight to the floor drain.

- Drain limiter = the six ¾ po tray ports (~90 L/min
  aggregate); 2 po main ~100 L/min, 1 po full-port valve
  ~130 L/min. 500 L water flood drains in ~6 min.
