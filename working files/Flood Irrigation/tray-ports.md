# Tray drain + overflow ports — one design, every tray, every zone

Two bulkhead ports per tray, both Active Aqua ¾ po
fill/drain fittings through the tray floor. The drop below the
port (vinyl, valve, tee into the zone line) is zone-specific: `nursery-racks.md`, `nursery-table.md`,
`greenhouse-table.md`.

- **Drain port**: fitting + screen (inside). Flush with the
  floor → complete drain-back.

- **Overflow port**: fitting + 2 extensions ≈ 2,5 po
  standpipe + screen on top. Caps the plain-water flood;
  feed flood (1 po, timed) never reaches it.

- Both take a ¾ po vinyl drop on the barb, 1 inox gear
  clamp — drop + clamps are in each zone's drop table.

- Shim the bulkheads with aquarium silicone (`common.md`
  § Bonus) → no puddle around the flange.
