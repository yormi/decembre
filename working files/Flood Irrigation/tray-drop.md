# Tray fill drop — one design, every tray, every zone

Header at 0,7 bar (10 psi regulator, `common.md` § Shared
design) → punch-in barbed valve on the header → 1,4 m MIXC
¼ po tube (the fixed orifice, ~3,3 L/min) → top-hat grommet
¼ po through the tray wall (3/8 po hole, above flood line)
→ barbed elbow ¼ po on the tube end inside, aimed down.

- Barbed valve = take-off AND per-tray shutoff (empty tray
  on single-pass = ~20-30 L feed dumped/day).

- Tube seats on barbs with a hot-water dip; no clamps.

- Grommet holds the tube; tube pulls out for cleaning.

- Punch 3 mm, room-temperature pipe; goof plug fixes a bad
  hole.

- One length, 1,4 m, every tray in every zone. No trim,
  no calibration. Shelf height on the racks costs ~10 %
  flow at the top; the zone timer absorbs it.

- Racks: zip-tie the tube to the shelf as strain relief.

- Commissioning: jug + stopwatch 30 s on one drop → set the
  zone fill timer from that flow.
