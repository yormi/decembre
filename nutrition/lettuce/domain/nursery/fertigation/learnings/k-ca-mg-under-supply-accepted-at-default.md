## K / Ca / Mg under-supply accepted at default (2026-05-09)

Default delivers ~88 mg K/tray ≈ 2 % of 3 780 demand; Ca 0; Mg 0. No
REQ-K-supply spec today. Accepted on the assumption peat starter is
calcitic-limed and supplies the residual (cert 1 — investigate). Refinement
path: tissue test on 5-week trays at transplant. <2 % Ca or <0.4 % Mg → source
soluble organic Ca-Mg supplement. Production LETTUCE recipe is the
post-transplant backstop.

## Compromise closed (2026-08-25)

The 5-week balance at the 50 g target (`working files/nursery-5wk-balance.md`)
quantified the front-load and disproved the "calcitic peat covers the
residual" assumption (K −65 %, Ca −42 %, Mg −24 %). Default recipe rebalanced:
K/Mg by mined sulfates, Ca by gypsum in the bucket, N/P cut to exact residual,
kelp dropped (dominant Na source), micros by stock bottle, 5 buckets/week at
2 L/tray retained volume. This learning is historical record only.
