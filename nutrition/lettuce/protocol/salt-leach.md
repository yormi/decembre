# Protocol — field bed salt leach (clean-water flush)

Hold root-zone salinity under the sulphur guardrail without pausing the sulphur
program. The salt is saline (Ca-sulfate + nitrate), not sodic → it leaches with
clean water. Basis: `../domain/learnings/field-sme-salinity-climbed-2026-06.md`.
EC method: `soil-ph-ec-test.md`.

**Question it closes:** is the root zone back under ECe 1.3 before the next
sulphur pass / transplant?

Scale: salanova bed, 30″ × 100 ft = 23.2 m² top.


## Trigger

- Before every sulphur pass and before transplant → read 1:1 slurry EC.

- Leach when slurry EC > ~0.65 (≈ ECe 1.3; factor ~2).

- Backstop: leach on the June SME footing (CE 4.01) until a fresh read clears it.


## Steps

1. Read 1:1 slurry EC + pH first (`soil-ph-ec-test.md`) → log bed, date.

2. Apply clean water only, no feed → until ~half of it drains past the root
   zone (leaching fraction ~0.5).

3. Drain freely → never leave the bed waterlogged (S⁰ → H₂S root burn).

4. Dry back to workable → re-read slurry EC.

5. Repeat until slurry EC ≤ 0.65 (ECe ≤ 1.3).

6. Only then run the sulphur pass / transplant.


## Notes

- From CE 4.01, one pass won't reach band → several passes, spaced with dry-back
  between (occluded salt diffuses out slowly).

- Leach **before** the pass, not instead of it. The program continues; the leach
  holds EC under the salt-sensitive guardrail.

- Clean water only. Gypsum/Ca is pointless here — the soil is already
  Ca-saturated and the problem is total salt, not sodium.

- Calibrate volume by the drain: read EC of what runs off; when drain EC ≈ input
  water EC, the pass has stopped pulling salt.
