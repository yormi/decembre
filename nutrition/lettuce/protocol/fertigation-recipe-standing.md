# Lettuce field fertigation — standing recipe (B + K only)

Self-contained handoff sheet. Salanova field beds, organic
(CAN/CGSB-32.311). Tank carries only the two nutrients the soil can't
supply fast enough; everything else comes from the soil bank + the
pre-plant front-load.

Scale basis: **136.8 m²** planted (4.5 beds × 30.4 m²), harvest
~**100 kg fresh/wk**, weekly batch.


## Recipe (weekly)

| Product | Weekly amount |
|---|---|
| Sulfate de potassium 0-0-50 (K₂SO₄) | **400 g** |
| Solubore (20,5 % B) | **0,7 g** |

- Stock: **24 L** barrel, injected at **2 %** (Dosatron).

- Weigh the 0,7 g boron on a **0,1 g scale**.


## Why only these two

Both are the nutrients whose soil resupply can't keep pace with a
year of harvest:

- **Potassium** — soil bank is healthy (645 kg/ha) but mass flow
  covers only ~4 % of peak uptake; the rest rides on slow diffusion.
  Weekly removal ≈ 350 g K (≈ 840 g K₂SO₄). Dosed at **400 g** — a
  CE-safe partial (~+0,5 mS/cm) on a salt-stressed crop. The bank
  covers the gap ~48 wk → **monitor tissue K, top up if it dips.**

- **Boron** — soil B is **below detection**; the tank is the sole
  source. 0,7 g Solubore = weekly removal (~150 mg B).


## Deliberately excluded

- **Mg, Fe, Mn, Zn, Cu** — dropped 2026-07-11. At bed pH 6.4 the old
  lockout is relieved, so the soil resupplies these fast enough for
  lettuce's modest demand. Were tank maintenance-only.

- **Calcium / Phosphorus** — soil banks large; no clean
  organic-soluble tank source. Addressed by the soil-pH program.

- **Nitrogen** — supplied by the pre-plant front-load (alfalfa +
  actisol + feather meal), not this tank.

- **Sodium molybdate** — soil Mo in-spec; adds sodium; dropped.

- **Citric acid** — no benefit on clean, mildly acidic water; dropped.

- The 400 g K is a salinity-capped partial, not full replacement —
